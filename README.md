# 📊 Sales & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard

### Enterprise-Grade Business Intelligence Solution Built with Microsoft Power BI

Transforming **10,000 Sales Transactions** and **500 Customer Records** into actionable executive insights through interactive dashboards, advanced DAX, forecasting, and strategic business recommendations.

---

# 📖 Executive Summary

Business leaders need fast, reliable, and interactive insights to make informed decisions. Traditional spreadsheets often make it difficult to identify performance trends, profitability drivers, and future opportunities.

This project demonstrates how modern Business Intelligence can transform raw transactional data into an executive decision-support system using **Power BI**, **Power Query**, **DAX**, and **Star Schema Data Modeling**.

The dashboard provides descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics through five interactive report pages.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The organization needed a centralized dashboard capable of answering questions such as:

- Which regions generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories contribute the most profit?
- Where are profit margins declining?
- What are the monthly sales trends?
- How is business expected to perform in the coming months?
- Which strategic actions should management prioritize?
